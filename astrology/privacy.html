Privacy policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

1. Who is responsible for your information

Iris Calder, an astrologer working from Asheville, North Carolina, USA (“I”, “me”). I decide how the information you share on this site is used. You can reach me at hello@iriscalder.example.

2. What I collect and why

  • The booking form: your name, how to contact you, the reading you chose, your date, time and place of birth and — only if you add them — your question and the birth details of a partner or child. I use this to prepare your reading and to reply to you. Legal basis (GDPR): taking steps at your request before a contract, and your consent for anything sensitive you choose to share.
  • Payment details are handled by the payment provider when you pay (for example Stripe or PayPal). I see that a payment was made and its amount, never your full card number.
  • Emails and messages you send me, so I can answer them.

I don’t run advertising, analytics trackers or social media pixels on this site, and I don’t use cookies to follow you around the web. Fonts are served from this site, not from Google.

3. How the booking form is protected

Before your form leaves your browser it is encrypted on your device (ECDH P-256 key agreement and AES-256-GCM). The server only ever stores the encrypted text; it can be opened only in my dashboard with my private key. The connection itself is protected with HTTPS.

Client notes in my dashboard are kept encrypted as well, and backups are encrypted before they’re stored.

4. What stays on your own device

The free tools — birth charts, “My charts”, lesson progress, your theme and recent searches — are calculated and saved in your browser only (local storage). They aren’t sent to me. You can delete them at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

When you search for a city, the name you type is sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding service to find its coordinates and time zone. Nothing else is sent with it.

5. Who else may process it

  • my hosting and email providers, to run the site and deliver messages;
  • the payment provider you pay through;
  • the video or voice tool we agree on for the session (for example Zoom or Google Meet).

I don’t sell your personal information and I don’t share it for advertising. Some providers are based in the United States; where the law requires it, they offer standard contractual clauses for transfers from the EU and UK.

6. How long I keep it

  • Inquiries that don’t lead to a reading — deleted within 90 days.
  • Client records and session notes — up to 3 years after our last session, so a follow-up reading can build on the first one. Ask me and I’ll delete them sooner.
  • Payment and tax records — as long as US tax law requires (generally up to 7 years).

7. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask me to show you what I hold about you, correct it, delete it, or send you a copy. If you’re in the EU or the UK (GDPR), you can also object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent at any time, and complain to your data protection authority. If you’re a California resident (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to know, delete and correct your information and not to be discriminated against for using these rights — and, again, I don’t sell or share it.

Write to hello@iriscalder.example. I reply within 30 days, usually much sooner.

8. Sensitive topics

Readings sometimes touch on health, relationships or family. Share only what you’re comfortable with — astrology is a tool for reflection and doesn’t replace medical, psychological, legal or financial advice.

9. Children

This site isn’t aimed at children under 16. A parent or guardian may book a reading about their child and provides the child’s birth details on their behalf.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date. Significant changes will be pointed out on the home page.

This is a demo site by Nevaro Studio. Iris Calder is a fictional astrologer, and the booking form here never leaves your browser. A live client’s policy is adapted to their country, payment providers and tools, and they review it before launch.
