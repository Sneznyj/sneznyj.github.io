Approach

Looking good is the easy part. A site has four jobs.

  1. 01

    Get found

    Pages are prerendered, light and fast, with clean titles, structured data and a sitemap — readable by search engines and AI assistants alike. On launch we connect Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.

  2. 02

    Earn trust in ten seconds

    Real photos, a plain price list, an about page in your own voice and a privacy policy that matches what the site actually does. No stock smiles, no invented reviews.

  3. 03

    Make booking effortless

    A short intake form that understands birth dates, birth times and time zones. It lands in your dashboard already sorted, so you don’t copy details out of DMs.

  4. 04

    Give people a reason to come back

    Free tools your clients use between sessions — their chart, the moon calendar, today’s sky. Every visit is a quiet reminder that you exist.

Under the hood

Built properly, so you never have to think about it

Private by design

Booking forms are encrypted in the visitor’s browser before they’re sent. The server stores ciphertext only; names and birth data open only in your dashboard.

Secure hosting

Static hosting with HTTPS everywhere and nothing on the server to break into. Domain and SSL renewals are on our list, not yours.

Backups you can restore

Your client data syncs between your devices encrypted, and you can download a full backup in one click.

Fast on a phone

Self-hosted fonts, compressed images, no page builders and no tracking scripts by default.

Accessible

Readable contrast (WCAG AA), keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support and real text instead of text in images.

Accurate

Astrology calculations are tested against Swiss Ephemeris: planets agree within 20 arcseconds, angles and house cusps within 4.

Questions

Before you write

Do I own the site?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, you get the full source code, and nothing is locked into a page builder or our account.

Where is it hosted?

On static hosting such as GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages or Netlify — fast, usually free, with nothing on the server to hack. If your booking inbox needs a server, we deploy a small one on Cloudflare.

Can I change texts and prices myself?

Yes. Services, prices, FAQ and contacts live in one plain file with comments next to every field. Or send us a message under the care plan.

How accurate are the charts?

Planet positions come from astronomy-engine (based on NASA JPL data). We test them against Swiss Ephemeris: planets agree within 20 arcseconds, angles and house cusps within 4, the true node within one arcminute.

What do you need from me?

Your photos (or we help plan a shoot), your services and prices, and an hour of your time to talk through texts. If you already have a domain, its login.

Can my clients pay on the site?

Yes — through a payment link from Stripe, PayPal or your local provider. We connect the provider you already use; card details never touch your site.

What if I want changes after launch?

Small fixes in the first 30 days are on us. After that — the care plan or a separate quote.
